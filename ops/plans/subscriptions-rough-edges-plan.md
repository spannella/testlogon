# Subscriptions — Rough-Edges Audit & Remediation Plan

**Subsystem:** creator recurring subscriptions ("plans" in code)
**Audited on:** dev clone `~/dev/testlogon` @ `android-impl` HEAD `1d5ab85c` (byte-identical prod==dev for `subscription_server.py`)
**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Scope of audit:** what is ROUGH after the SUB-E0..E5 completion program shipped (real charge, renewal+dunning, proration/trials/gifting/cancel+refund, subscriber-only gating, subscriber-mgmt+MRR, notifications). This is an AUDIT deliverable — the only write is this plan. No subscriptions behavior was changed.
**Respects LOCKED DECISIONS** (real-charge renewals via funds-guarded rail, phantom grandfathered, gift=one cycle, trial=7d card-up-front, 10% fee, proration charge-delta up / schedule down, cancel at-period-end default, binary gating, grace 7d, dunning d1/3/5/7, expiring-soon 3d).

---

## 1. HEADLINE VERDICT

**Subscriptions is a genuinely real recurring-revenue engine with a soft, leaky perimeter.** The core rail the E-program built is sound: E0 subscribe charges real money before any write; the sweeper renews, dunning-declines, and expires on the funds-guarded rail with per-cycle idempotency; gift is correctly one-cycle; trial auto-convert charges; the reversal→clawback ledger mechanics are correct; and the binary "any active sub" gate enforces on all 6 surfaces and re-locks on expiry. That is a polished core.

**But the completion program re-plumbed the PRIMARY paths and left the SECONDARY paths on the pre-E0 stubs, and the whole subscriber SELF-SERVICE loop on mobile is thin.** The rough edges are load-bearing and cluster in five spots:

1. **Money leaks through the endpoints E0/E2 never re-plumbed.** The manual `/trial/convert` credits real withdrawable dollars with NO charge (phantom revenue); the standalone `/refund` lets a subscriber refund their own cycle AND keep access; the billing webhook is UNAUTHENTICATED and can un-expire/extend any sub for free and defeat dunning. These are directly exploitable with only the caller's own user id (or none).
2. **Proration is computed against `start_at` (whole subscription lifetime) instead of the current cycle** — so every upgrade under-charges and every cancel under-refunds on any sub that has renewed even once, worsening with tenure. Plus cross-interval (month↔year) upgrade/downgrade is classified by raw price and mischarges.
3. **The subscriber's mobile self-service loop dead-ends repeatedly.** No "My Subscriptions" list (the manage screen silently shows ONE sub, and its "Manage" button can open the WRONG creator); card-less users are told to "add a card" with no route; and a PAST_DUE (dunning) sub has NO action button at all — the exact window the "update your card" notification points at is inert.
4. **Per-tier gating is not merely deferred — a complete tier-level system exists (`fan_club_*`) and is abandoned/disjoint** from the subscription `PLAN#` model, so a $2 subscriber unlocks $50-tier content on all 6 surfaces, and `subscriber_only` can't be edited after create (post or broadcast).
5. **Reporting lies after refunds, and the creator console is split-brained.** `/earnings` and E4 analytics read the wrong table for refunds (so net/MRR overstate after any cancel/dispute) and count gifts + non-renewing subs into MRR; Android has MRR + subscriber-mgmt but NO tier authoring, while web has tier authoring but NO MRR/subscriber console — neither platform is a complete creator console.

**Completeness, honestly:** the money ENGINE is ~85% real (core rail correct; secondary endpoints + reporting rough). The SUBSCRIBER FLOW on mobile is ~55% — you can subscribe and get gated content, but you can't see all your subs, can't recover from dunning, and hit dead-ends without a saved card. The CREATOR FLOW is ~50% and platform-split — no mobile authoring, no web console. GATING is functionally correct at the binary level but the tier dimension the product implies is entirely absent. NOTIFICATIONS: the 7 transactional types fire and deep-link correctly on Android, but the recovery deep-link lands on an inert screen, several lifecycle transitions are silent, and every web deep-link is wrong or dead. Verdict: **a polished core with a rough, leaky perimeter — not a finished product.**

### Recommended build sequence
1. **X0 (SUBX-01→03)** — stop the P0 money leaks immediately (independent, small).
2. **X1 (SUBX-10→15)** — money correctness (proration, intervals, real remove-refund, dunning cadence, discounts, guards).
3. **X2 (SUBX-20→24)** — subscriber mobile self-service (My-subs, correct-target Manage, add-card + PAST_DUE recovery, reactivate honesty, discovery/gift/polish).
