# MON-003 gaps

- [HIGH] Subscription revenue is invisible to the creator earnings dashboard — subscription server writes creator revenue under `PK=CREATOR#{creator_id}` in `T.subscriptions` (`app/routers/subscription_server.py:582,947`), but `_query_credit_entries` queries `PK=USER#{user_id}` in `T.billing` (`app/services/creator_earnings.py:144`) — subscription income never appears in `GET /ui/earnings/summary` even though it is the dominant revenue stream for most creators — Fix: update `record_billing_payment` / `save_ledger_entry` to also write a `type=credit` LEDGER entry under `PK=USER#{creator_id}` in `T.billing`; or update `_query_credit_entries` to additionally query the subscriptions table under `CREATOR#` key — Effort: M

- [MED] `get_available_balance` in `creator_payouts.py` does not filter out `state=reversed` credit entries — `app/services/creator_payouts.py:84-90` — a credit entry for a chargebacked subscription or refunded tip remains in the balance calculation, causing the available payout amount to be inflated; creators could withdraw funds that were reversed — Fix: add `FilterExpression=Attr("type").eq("credit") & Attr("state").ne("reversed")` to the billing ledger query — Effort: S

- [LOW] `_query_credit_entries` caps iteration at 20 pages of 500 items (10,000 entries) with a hard break — `app/services/creator_earnings.py:155-165` — high-volume creators with >10,000 ledger entries receive silently truncated summaries with no indication that data was capped — Fix: add a `truncated: bool` field to `EarningsSummaryOut` and set it when the page limit is hit; log a warning with the creator's user_id — Effort: S
